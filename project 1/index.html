How far ahead can I pre-order?

Up to three days. Orders close an hour before your pickup slot.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Our oat flat white and olive oil cake are vegan. We bake in a shared kitchen, so we can’t guarantee gluten-free.

Can you cater an event?

Yes. Pastry boxes and bread platters for 10 to 60 people. Ask at the counter or call us.
